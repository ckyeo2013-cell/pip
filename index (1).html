Pip lives on your desktop.

A friendly 3D sugar glider that hops around your screen, follows your cursor, chats, helps you decide, and can do things on your computer. It always asks before it does.

What Pip does

  • Click Pip to chat. Drag Pip anywhere; it glides back to the bottom of your screen.
  • Ask for help with writing, code, maths, plans or decisions.
  • Every action on your computer appears as a card showing exactly what it will do. Nothing happens until you click Approve.
  • Pip only blocks clicks where Pip itself is. The rest of your screen works normally.

Installing

Windows says "Windows protected your PC"

Pip isn't code-signed yet. Click More info, then Run anyway.

Mac says the app can't be opened

Open the .dmg and drag Pip to Applications. Then right-click Pip, choose Open, and confirm. You only need to do this once.

Linux

Make the file runnable with chmod +x Pip-*.AppImage, then double-click it or run it from a terminal.
